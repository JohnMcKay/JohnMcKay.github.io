About

I am a research scientist at Amazon Transport Sciences, where I focus on applying machine learning to freight and inbound transportation problems, including lane‑level forecasting, contract pricing, and shipper willingness‑to‑pay modeling.

Previously, I worked on Alexa, designing NLP systems and neural embeddings to improve entity resolution. I earned my PhD from Penn State University under the advisement of Dr. Vishal Monga, focusing on synthetic aperture image formation and classification with neural networks.
