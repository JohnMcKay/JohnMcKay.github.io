About

Principal Data Scientist at Microsoft Industry Solutions Engineering (Studio 1–3), creating high-impact AI tools for the largest enterprise Azure clients with a focus on the financial industry.

Previously: research scientist at Amazon Transport Sciences, applying machine learning to Amazon Freight / inbound transport service problems (lane-level forecasting, contract pricing, shipper willingness-to-pay, cost adjustments). I also applied skills to Alexa: NLP, neural embeddings, design, fixing bugs, improving entity resolution.

PhD from Penn State University (advisor: Dr. Vishal Monga) focused on synthetic aperture image formation and classification with neural networks. My background includes electrical engineering and mathematical biology; undergraduate and master’s research spanned applied mathematics and epidemiological / network modeling.

If you see anything on the website and want to know more, contact me.
